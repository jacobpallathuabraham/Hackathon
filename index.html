    Questions

    Frequently asked

    When is the application deadline?

    Fall 2026 applications close on November 30. Spring applications close on December 15.

    What English score do I need?

    IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 79 or EmSAT English 1250 for most undergraduate programmes.

    Is support available for students with learning differences?

    Yes. Student Accessibility Services offers confidential support, exam accommodations and ERLY, a consent-first study companion. Sign in through Option 2 to explore it.

    Can I live on campus?

    Yes — residences are available for first-year and international students, with separate halls for men and women.

    Do you offer transfer credit?

    Up to 50% of a degree can be transferred from accredited institutions.
