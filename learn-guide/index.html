Learning paths

  • Human and plant biology foundations (Biology, Secondary / NEET foundation) — How the body moves oxygen around, and how plants make the glucose we all depend on. Lessons in order: The heart and double circulation → Photosynthesis and limiting factors
  • Ready for calculus (Mathematics, Secondary → pre-university) — Parabolas, roots and turning points, then the derivative that finds them. Lessons in order: Quadratic functions and their graphs → The derivative as a rate of change
  • Forces and circuits (Physics, Secondary / JEE foundation) — Newton’s second law, then current, voltage and resistance. Lessons in order: Newton’s second law: force and acceleration → Current, voltage and resistance
  • Chemistry foundations (Chemistry, Secondary → JEE) — Acids, bases and pH, then why molecules have the shapes they do. Lessons in order: Acids, bases and the pH scale → Shapes of molecules (VSEPR)
  • Lines, then motion (Physics, Secondary) — The gradient of a straight line, then what gradients and areas mean on motion graphs. Lessons in order: Straight lines: linear functions → Describing motion: graphs of movement

Addresses

Every place above has a stable address. In the Studio, routes follow #/; the same places also open from query strings, which survive tools that drop the # part.

  • learn.html?subject=physics — a subject world (also maths, chemistry, biology, cs, english, geography, history, languages, social, more; “mathematics” and “computer-science” work too)
  • learn.html?lesson=phys-newton and learn.html?topic=phys-newton — a lesson and its topic page
  • learn.html?exp=projectile — an interactive experience
  • learn.html?section=lab — a Studio section
  • learn.html?inspect=1 — this directory, read-only, no sign-in. Add any of the above, or any #/ route, to open that place read-only.
  • learn-guide/ — the same directory as plain HTML files that need no JavaScript.

Without inspect=1 the Studio asks for the demo sign-in first, then opens the requested place.
