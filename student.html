01The gap

Two official numbers that can't both be the whole story.

0%

of UAE Grade 12 students pass national literacy benchmarks.

Bayanat open data · Grade 12 pass rates, 2012–2021

vs

0%

of Emirati female students screened at UAE University showed dyslexic features — in Arabic and English.

Aboudan et al., 2011 · IJEL 1(2):64

School exams measure whether you can read. University measures whether you can keep reading 40 dense pages a week, in your second language, at 1am. That is where masked difficulties surface — and where nobody is currently looking.

Our own critique of our own evidence

  • 2007/08 data — almost two decades old
  • One university, female students only
  • Screening features, not clinical diagnoses

That isn't a hole in the argument. It is the argument: the most-cited UAE university figure is 17 years old because no one has measured since. Our pilot produces the first current, consented dataset.

02How it works

No tests. Just studying, with someone quietly paying attention.

ERLY replaces one-off screening with something that fits real student life. It follows indicators while you study for fourteen days, measured only against your own normal, and then it stops.

  1. 01

    You say yes

    Pick which categories ERLY may notice. The agreement shows a start date, an end date, and a stop-and-delete button.

  2. 02

    You study as usual

04The Reading Studio

Every reading, made to fit you.

Open any course PDF in a calm, full-screen studio. Change the font, size and spacing. Have it read aloud in the voice you like. Tap any highlighted word for its meaning in English and Arabic. If a paragraph is fighting back, Noor, your study buddy, gently offers to work through it with you.

  • 5 fonts, size, line, letter and word spacing, line length, 6 page tints
  • Read-aloud with any voice on your device, speed and pitch, word-by-word highlight
  • Noor explains in plain words, asks a friendly check question, and walks through diagrams
  • Glossary with Arabic meanings, line focus, word anchors, a breathing break

05Your insight

A picture, not a label.

After day 14, every indicator comes together in a page made for you. It shows your fortnight as weather, what might be getting in the way, what stayed steady, and what helps.

06The hand-off

From a private insight to a real appointment, with someone beside you.

Fourteen days of indicators can build evidence strong enough to justify a referral. Only a professional assessment can confirm a learning disability, and SAS does that part.

A

Your Evidence Pack

Your fortnight summary: areas flagged, the indicators that stayed above your normal, and the dates. You download it, and you decide who sees it.

B

Bring an ally

Invite someone you trust. They get a short Support Playbook — what to say, what to avoid — never your scores.

Try saying

“Thanks for telling me. Want me to come with you?”

Please avoid

“You just need to try harder.”

C

Book SAS

One tap. Your intake is pre-filled from the Evidence Pack, your ally can attend, and you get a check-in 48 hours later.

Don't want to be tracked? You don't have to be.

07Privacy by design

The best privacy feature is a finish line.

We never collect

  • Anything you type
  • Camera or webcam — ever
  • Voice recordings (naming is processed on-device)
  • Emotion or facial analysis
  • Location
  • Anything after day 14
What changed, and why

From always-on to two weeks

Semester-long passive monitoring was our biggest weakness. Now tracking happens only with consent, only for 14 days, and only against your own normal. Raw events are deleted once the summary is written.

“A false positive costs a workshop invite, not a label. A false negative still has an always-open self-referral door.”

Aligned with

  • PDPL — Federal Decree-Law 45/2021Consent: specific, time-limited, revocable.
  • UAE Charter for the Development & Use of AI (2024)A human confirms every outcome.
  • National Policy for Empowering People of Determination (2017)Early identification and inclusion.
  • UAE AI Strategy 2031AI for education and wellbeing.

09Hard questions

Ask us anything. We already asked ourselves.

Can ERLY diagnose dyslexia or ADHD?

No, and it never says it can. Fourteen days of indicators can justify a referral. A professional assessment at SAS confirms.

Isn't tracking students creepy?

That's why it is opt-in, limited to the categories you choose, shown as a countdown, and stoppable at any moment. It switches off on day 14 and deletes the raw data.

Do you read what I type?

Never. ERLY sees events, such as a scroll back, a deleted draft, or a timestamp. It never sees content.

Why compare me only with myself?

A naturally slow reader isn't struggling, just different. ERLY looks for change against your own normal, never a class average.

What does Noor do with my answers?

Noor's questions help you understand the reading, and they feed a few help-seeking indicators on your device. They are not a test, and nobody else sees them.

What about exam weeks?

Exam Mode expects heavier days, so normal exam stress isn't read as a pattern.

Why Arabic and English?

If friction appears only on English pages, it may be language load. If it appears in both languages, it's more likely a reading difficulty. ERLY tracks the gap.

Is this buildable?

The Reading Studio, Noor and the live indicators run for real in this demo. The LMS feed and on-device summaries are simulated and labelled as such.
