Handwriting Check

Copy three short sentences — on screen with your finger, a stylus or the mouse, or on paper with a photo. Text-recognition software running on this device tries to read them back and shows which words it recognised.

What it does
  • Tries to read your writing with open-source text recognition (Tesseract), inside this page
  • Shows which words were recognised and which weren’t
  • Says “inconclusive” when it can’t read enough
What it cannot do
  • Judge your handwriting, spelling, intelligence or personality
  • Detect dyslexia, dysgraphia, ADHD or any condition
  • Replace a conversation with Student Accessibility Services

🔒 Your writing and photos stay on this device: nothing is uploaded or saved, and Discard deletes them.
