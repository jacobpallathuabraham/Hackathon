Handwriting Check

Write by hand on screen or on paper. Text-recognition software in this page tries to read it back and tells you how sure it is.

🔒 Privacy: stays on this device
  • Your writing and photos are held in this page’s memory only. They are never uploaded and never saved to your device’s storage.
  • Recognition runs inside this page. The quick reader’s language files come from this website.
  • The optional handwriting reader (about 64 MB) is downloaded only if you press its button, from the Hugging Face model hub, and your browser keeps that model for next time. Your writing is not sent with it.
  • Only your display choices — paper, pen size, colour and guide — are remembered on this device.
  • Discard removes the strokes, undo history, photo, prepared image and recognised text from the page. Leaving the page drops them too.
What this can and cannot do
It can
  • Try to read your writing with open-source text recognition
  • Show which words it read clearly and which it was unsure about
  • Say “inconclusive” when it can’t read enough
It cannot
  • Judge your handwriting, spelling, intelligence or personality
  • Detect dyslexia, dysgraphia, ADHD or any condition
  • Replace a conversation with Student Accessibility Services
