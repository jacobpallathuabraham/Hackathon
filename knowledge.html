Coverage limitations

  • Wikipedia is the broadest integrated source; specialist domains (law, medicine, film, social platforms) rely on it until their connectors are added.
  • Live news needs either the opt-in Google Search grounding in the gateway (with its display rules) or a news connector; GDELT timed out during testing and is not integrated.
  • Sources needing keys (OpenAlex, TMDB, YouTube, NASA, FRED…) work only after a free key is added to the gateway — never to the website.
  • Paywalled publishers (Reuters, AP, Britannica, journals) are linked, never scraped.
  • Answers can only be as current and complete as the sources returned for each question; Noor says when a live source was not reached.
