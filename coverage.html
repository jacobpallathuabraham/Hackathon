What Noor can show — measured, not claimed

Each concept below has a real question a student might ask. When this page opens, every question is sent through Noor’s own routing in this browser (Noor Lab engines, the scene planner and the curated visual library) and the page records what would actually appear. Nothing here is typed in by hand, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Interactive: a computed, adjustable model. Visual: an interactive visual or animated scene. Diagram: a labelled diagram, flow or mind map. Explanation only: Noor answers in words (with an AI model when one is in use); no visual yet. This registry is a sample of concepts, not the whole of any curriculum.

Staged plan for what isn’t covered yet

  1. Now (v17): problem-to-scene engines for rotational mechanics, multi-charge electrostatics, area between curves, three organic mechanisms (SN1, SN2, E2), circuit topology, gear trains, ideal binary distillation, FM, the cardiac cycle and break-even analysis.
  2. Next: more mechanisms (electrophilic aromatic substitution, additions, carbonyl chemistry) from a checked mechanism data set; AC circuits with phasors; Fourier series; action potentials (Hodgkin–Huxley); enzyme kinetics; supply–demand with shifts.
  3. Later: non-ideal distillation (activity models, azeotropes); multi-body rotational problems with collisions; respiratory and renal models; accounting statements as interactive ledgers.
  4. Not planned as models: topics where a visual adds little or would mislead (law, ethics, literary analysis) stay as clear explanations and diagrams.
